# CivTech-Allocat

## Overview

CivTech-Allocat is a cloud-based workflow automation and case management platform that I designed and developed to streamline the administration of grant, scholarship, welfare, and programme management processes. The platform digitises the complete application lifecycle—from submission and eligibility assessment through verification, interviews, approvals, allocation, and reporting.

The system was created to replace manual, paper-based processes with a secure, scalable, and auditable digital solution that improves efficiency, transparency, and decision-making.

---

## Business Problem

Many grant and programme administrators rely on spreadsheets, emails, and manual processes to manage applications, making it difficult to:

* Process large application volumes
* Track assessment progress
* Verify supporting evidence
* Manage interviews and decisions
* Produce accurate reports
* Maintain audit trails
* Ensure consistent and transparent decision-making

CivTech-Allocat addresses these challenges through an integrated workflow management platform.

## Real-World Deployment

CivTech Allocat is not a concept or demonstration project. It was designed, developed, and deployed to support the operations of **Anthony Udokwu's Foundation–Yahweh**, a UK-registered charity delivering scholarships, welfare support, hardship relief, and community empowerment programmes.

The platform is used to manage:

- Online grant and scholarship applications
- Eligibility assessments
- Evidence verification
- Interview workflows
- Decision recording
- Applicant records
- Appeals and clarification requests
- Support ticket management
- Programme administration
- Reporting and audit trails
